# Receiving bank · evidence for the pitch

Checked 21 November 2026. The receiving bank has a role because it services the destination account and can investigate payments or related account activity under its own controls. Any intervention depends on the bank's authority and the relevant payment scheme.

## UK · a financial incentive on eligible claims

- Since 7 October 2024, the regime covers qualifying UK-to-UK Faster Payments and retail CHAPS APP scams. The maximum mandatory reimbursement is £85,000 per eligible claim. The sending PSP assesses and reimburses the victim first, then requests the attributable receiving-side contribution. For Faster Payments, the contribution is the lower of 50% of the eligible actual amount paid or 50% of the required eligible reimbursement attributable to that receiving PSP. Excess and multiple recipients can affect the amount. For a single receiving PSP and £10,000 of eligible reimbursement with no applicable excess, the illustrative contribution is £5,000. The CHAPS rules are broadly aligned. [PSR consumer scope](https://www.psr.org.uk/information-for-consumers/app-fraud-reimbursement-protections/) · [PSR PS25/5, §§3.19–3.26](https://www.psr.org.uk/media/rhelv4op/ps25-5-app-scams-reimbursement-consolidated-policy-statement-may-2025.pdf) · [Bank of England, CHAPS annual report](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/report/2025/rtgs-and-chaps-annual-report-2024-25)
- The PSR reported more than £74 million reimbursed to sending firms from October 2024 to June 2025. It also identified delays and reconciliation difficulties, including incorrect references. Separately, the PSR's historical Metric C reports the value of APP scams received per £1 million received and volume per million transactions for the covered sample. These are observed market practices and useful metric definitions. [PSR on contributions and reconciliation](https://www.psr.org.uk/news-and-updates/thought-pieces/thought-pieces/one-year-on-delivering-fair-outcomes-through-our-app-scams-reimbursement-regime/) · [PSR Metric C](https://www.psr.org.uk/app-fraud-data/)

## UAE · fraud response and scheme controls

- Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, effective 16 September 2025, requires licensed financial institutions to implement fraud prevention and detection, promptly notify affected customers of breaches or fraudulent incidents, take corrective action and cooperate with CBUAE investigations. Under Article 149(6), where reasonable concerns relate to a specific customer's transactions, a licensed institution may share with another authorized licensed institution only what is necessary for verification, subject to Article 147 confidentiality. These provisions support a bank-controlled investigation workflow. The pilot would need separate agreement on lawful sharing, scheme permission and actual bank actions. [Article 147](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/article-147-confidentiality-data-and-information) · [Article 149](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/article-149-fraud-prevention)
- CBUAE's Retail Payment Systems Regulation requires operational risk controls at designated payment systems and defined transfer, clearing and settlement rules. Its operator and settlement-institution obligations are distinct from obligations of banks providing accounts. Article 162 of the 2025 law protects finality in covered financial market infrastructures. Aani says it cannot accept amendments to instant instructions. State the possible outcomes as acknowledgement, investigation and bank-authorized actions under applicable scheme rules. The cited UAE provisions establish no equivalent to UK APP contribution sharing. [Retail Payment Systems Regulation](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/retail-payment-systems-regulation) · [DFL 6/2025, Arts. 157 and 162](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/federal-decree-law-no-6-2025-regarding-central-bank-regulation-financial-institutions-and) · [Aani terms](https://aep.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/)

## What a receiving-bank pilot can measure

- **Speed.** Median and P95 time from verified notice to acknowledgement and analyst disposition. Measure missing-field rate and untouched queue age alongside time.
- **Operational effort.** Analyst minutes per case, manual re-entry and reconciliation exceptions per 100 cases. Compare pilot-equivalent cohorts or replay identical synthetic cases through both workflows.
- **Risk and outcomes.** Confirmed scam receipts per million incoming payments and per million currency units received, plus false-positive manual referrals. Measure lawful actual returns and UK scheme contributions separately where available. The prototype can demonstrate timing and workflow completion on synthetic cases. Savings and loss reduction require controlled live evidence.
